# Deepfakes, Morphed Images, and Non-Consensual Imagery Guidance

## 1. Understanding the Threat
Image morphing and AI deepfakes involve superimposing a victim's face onto sexually explicit or compromising photos/videos without their consent. Perpetrators often use these fabricated media to blackmail victims for money (sextortion), demand favors, or tarnish social standing.

## 2. Immediate Crisis Response Checklist
1. **Remember: You Are the Victim, Not at Fault**: Morphed content and synthetic media are criminal fabrications created by perpetrators. Do not feel shame, guilt, or isolation.
2. **Do Not Pay Extortion or Ransom**:
   - Paying blackmailers never stops the extortion; it confirms that the threat works and leads to escalating demands for larger sums of money.
   - Cut off communication, but DO NOT delete the chat history.
3. **Preserve Complete Evidence**:
   - Capture clear screenshots of extortion messages, payment demands (bank account numbers, UPI IDs, cryptocurrency wallet addresses), timestamps, and profile usernames.
   - Record URLs where the content is hosted.
4. **Prevent Viral Spread with Hash-Based Takedown Services**:
   - **StopNCII.org**: Operated by the Revenge Porn Helpline and supported by major platforms (Meta, TikTok, OnlyFans, Bumble). Generates a cryptographic digital hash (hash value) directly on your device without ever uploading the actual image. Participating platforms block uploads matching that hash.
   - **Take It Down (takeitdown.ncmec.org)**: Operated by the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children for individuals under 18 or images taken before age 18.
5. **Report to Intermediaries under Rule 3(2)(b) of Indian IT Rules 2021**:
   - Social media platforms and web intermediaries in India are legally mandated by the IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules 2021 to remove non-consensual sexually explicit or morphed content within **24 hours** of receipt of a complaint from the victim or authorized person.
   - Contact the resident Grievance Officer of the platform via their official grievance email or web portal.
6. **Lodge an Official Police Report**:
   - File immediately at `https://cybercrime.gov.in` under "Report Crime Related to Women/Children".
   - Under Section 66E (Violation of privacy) and Section 67/67A of the IT Act (Publishing or transmitting sexually explicit material in electronic form), offenders face up to 5 to 7 years imprisonment.
