# Emergency Response Support System (ERSS 112) & 181 Women Helpline

## 1. What is ERSS 112?
ERSS (Emergency Response Support System) is the Pan-India single emergency response number for citizens in distress. It integrates all previously separated emergency lines (Police 100, Fire 101, Health 108) into a unified 24/7 service accessible via:
- Voice Call: Dial 112 from any mobile or landline phone (accessible even without cellular network balance or with locked phone in emergency call mode).
- SMS: Send an emergency SMS to 112.
- 112 India Mobile App: Available with a built-in panic button that transmits current GPS coordinates directly to the nearest State Emergency Response Centre (ERC).

### When to Call 112:
- Immediate physical threat, stalking, assault, domestic violence, or harassment in public transit or private spaces.
- Medical emergencies or accidents requiring prompt dispatch of first responders.
- Need for immediate police dispatch to a physical location.

### What Happens When You Call 112:
1. The call taker records your location, contact number, and nature of the emergency.
2. The system locates the nearest Emergency Response Vehicle (ERV) / PCR van via GPS.
3. The response vehicle is dispatched, and the caller receives an SMS with vehicle details and tracking link where available.

## 2. Women Helpline 181
181 is a toll-free 24/7 helpline dedicated to women affected by violence, both in private and public spaces. Supported by the Ministry of Women and Child Development (MWCD) under the Nirbhaya Fund.

### Scope and Services:
- Emergency response and rescue operations in coordination with local police and One Stop Centres (Sakhi Centres).
- Non-emergency counseling, psychological first aid, legal counseling, and medical assistance referrals.
- Information about government women welfare schemes, shelter homes, and legal aid clinics.
- Note on Availability: While 181 is operational across most Indian States and Union Territories, local state administration connects callers to the respective state command center. In cases of immediate life-threatening physical danger, calling 112 is always prioritized for immediate police mobilization.

## 3. One Stop Centres (Sakhi Centres)
Established across Indian districts to provide integrated support to women affected by violence under one roof:
- Medical examination and first aid.
- Assistance in lodging FIR / police complaint.
- Psycho-social support and trauma counseling.
- Legal counseling and representation through State Legal Services Authorities.
- Temporary shelter (up to 5 days) for women in acute distress.
