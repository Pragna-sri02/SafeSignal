# Official Account Recovery and Protection Guide

## 1. Compromised or Hacked Instagram Account
If your Instagram account has been hacked, your email or password was changed, or 2-factor authentication was hijacked by an attacker:

### Immediate Official Recovery Steps:
1. **Visit Official Recovery Portal**: Open `https://www.instagram.com/hacked` directly in your browser on a device you previously used to log into the account.
2. **Select Account Condition**:
   - "My account was hacked"
   - "I forgot my password"
   - "The login code was sent to a mobile number or email that I don't have access to"
   - "Someone used my name, photos or information to create a new account"
3. **Request a Login Link or Security Code**: Enter your username, original email address, or phone number.
4. **Identity Verification**:
   - If the account contains photos of you, Instagram provides a **Video Selfie Verification** feature where you submit a short head-movement video to prove you match profile pictures.
   - You can also nominate two Instagram friends to verify your identity.
5. **Security Email Reversion**: Check your email inbox for an official message from `security@mail.instagram.com`. If your email was recently modified, Instagram sends a notification containing a link saying *"revert this change"* or *"secure your account here"*.

## 2. Google / Gmail Compromised Account
If your Google account is compromised or you notice unrecognized logins:
1. Navigate directly to `https://accounts.google.com/signin/recovery`.
2. Answer security verification questions as accurately as possible from a familiar Wi-Fi network and device.
3. Review `https://myaccount.google.com/device-activity` to immediately sign out unrecognized sessions and devices.
4. Review third-party apps connected to your Google account at `https://myaccount.google.com/permissions` and revoke suspicious tokens.
5. Enable 2-Step Verification using an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator) or security keys, rather than SMS which is vulnerable to SIM swap.

## 3. WhatsApp Account Takeover & SIM Swap
If you suddenly lose cellular reception or WhatsApp alerts that your phone number is being used on another phone:
1. Re-register your phone number on WhatsApp immediately by entering your phone number and 6-digit SMS verification code.
2. If the hacker set up a 2-step verification PIN you don't know, WhatsApp places a mandatory 7-day waiting period before the PIN can be reset, but re-entering the SMS code immediately kicks the hacker off their session.
3. Notify close contacts via alternative channels that your WhatsApp is temporarily compromised to prevent the scammer from asking friends for emergency money.

## 4. Fundamental Rules for Account Recovery:
- **Never pay third-party "hackers" or "recovery agents"**: Any social media user claiming "DM me on Instagram to recover your hacked account" is a secondary recovery scammer.
- **Never share OTPs, recovery backup codes, or authenticator secrets** with anyone, even someone claiming to be "support".
- **Official platforms NEVER ask for passwords over chat or phone**.
