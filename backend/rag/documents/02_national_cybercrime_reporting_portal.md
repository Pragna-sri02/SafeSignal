# National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in)

## 1. Overview
The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (https://cybercrime.gov.in) is an initiative of the Government of India to facilitate victims/complainants to report cybercrime complaints online. It acts as a central clearinghouse where complaints are automatically routed to the designated Cyber Police Station in the victim's state or district.

## 2. Specialized Women and Child Cyber Crime Reporting Section
The portal features a dedicated section specifically for reporting cyber crimes against women and children:
- **Report Anonymously**: Allows victims or concerned citizens to report incidents involving child sexual abuse material (CSAM), non-consensual sharing of intimate images, morphed photos, or sexually explicit content without disclosing their identity.
- **Report & Track**: Requires basic registration (name, mobile number, state) and provides an acknowledgement number / tracking ID to track investigation progress and police action.

### Eligible Incident Categories under Women/Child Section:
- Morphing or deepfake creation using personal images.
- Non-consensual transmission or publication of obscene or sexually explicit content.
- Online cyberstalking, harassment, and persistent abusive communications.
- Impersonation, fake profiles created in a victim's name to damage reputation.
- Sextortion, blackmail, and online financial or personal extortion.

## 3. Financial Cyber Fraud Helpline: 1930
For cybercrimes involving financial fraud, unauthorized banking transactions, or extortion:
- Dial **1930** (formerly 155260) immediately, preferably within the "golden hour" (first 2-4 hours after transaction).
- The Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting and Management System works with banks, payment aggregators, and wallets to freeze defrauded amounts in transit before criminals can withdraw them.

## 4. Key Documents Required When Filing a Complaint
To ensure strong police action, prepare the following evidence:
- Full URL of the abusive post, profile, video, or group.
- Screenshots of offending content, abusive messages, emails, or threat texts showing the date, timestamp, and sender handles clearly.
- Suspect's phone number, email address, profile username, or UPI ID if known.
- Bank statement / UPI transaction reference ID (UTR) in case of extortion or ransom demands.
- Detailed written statement describing the chronology of events in plain language.
