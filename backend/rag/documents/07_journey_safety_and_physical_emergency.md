# Safe Travel Practices, Journey Monitoring, and Physical Safety

## 1. Journey Safety & Check-in Protocol
When traveling late at night, utilizing commercial cabs, or commuting through unfamiliar transit routes:
1. **Pre-Trip Planning & Notification**:
   - Inform trusted contacts of your destination, intended route, and estimated arrival time.
   - Use the SafeSignal Journey Safety feature to set an expected arrival time and periodic check-in reminder (e.g. every 15 or 30 minutes).
2. **Permission-Based Location Sharing**:
   - Share real-time location transparently with designated trusted contacts only.
   - SafeSignal does not perform covert background tracking; location sharing is strictly user-authorized and active only during an active journey or emergency SOS trigger.
3. **Transit Verification Safeguards**:
   - When taking ride-hail cabs (Ola, Uber, auto-rickshaws), verify the license plate number, driver identity, and vehicle model before entering.
   - Ensure the rear child-locks are disengaged on commercial vehicles.
   - Speak on a phone call or pretend to be on a call announcing the vehicle registration number and route aloud to deter misconduct.

## 2. Managing an Unsafe Transit Situation
If the driver takes an unexpected detour, locks doors, behaves erratically, or makes you feel unsafe:
- **Stay Alert and Calm**: Avoid escalating confrontation while inside a moving vehicle.
- **Instruct Clearly**: Firmly tell the driver: *"Please stop here immediately at this shop/gas station; my friend is waiting for me."*
- **Activate SafeSignal Emergency SOS**:
  - Triggers an instant alert record with your current location coordinates.
  - Generates notifications to configured trusted contacts.
  - Presents one-touch direct dial to India Police Emergency 112.
- **Seek Public Lit Areas**: Move towards metro stations, active petrol bunks, 24-hour pharmacies, or police kiosks.

## 3. Missed Check-ins & Escalation Workflow
If an expected arrival time passes without a manual check-in:
1. SafeSignal flags the journey status as **Overdue** and sends a prompt reminder to the traveler.
2. If no check-in or cancellation is logged within the configured grace period, the platform initiates the escalation workflow:
   - Configured emergency contacts receive a status alert indicating the traveler has not checked in at the designated destination.
   - Contact details, last recorded coordinates, and intended destination are shared with authorized recipients.
